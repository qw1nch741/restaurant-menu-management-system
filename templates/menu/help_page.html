Project Overview

A Comprehensive Restaurant Management System

About the Project

This Restaurant Management System is a backend-focused web application designed to streamline kitchen operations. It allows restaurant owners to manage their staff (cooks), categorize their menu items (dish types), and maintain an up-to-date digital menu (dishes) with ease.

Key Features

  • Dynamic Dashboard: Real-time statistics showing the total number of cooks, dishes, and categories.
  • Staff Management: Detailed profiles for cooks including years of experience and specific dish assignments.
  • Menu Organization: Effortless categorization of dishes into groups like Appetizers or Main Courses.
  • Secure Access: Full authentication system with secure login and logout protocols.
